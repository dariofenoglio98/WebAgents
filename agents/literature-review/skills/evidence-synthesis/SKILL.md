# Evidence Synthesis

Group papers by meaningful methodological relationships and report differences supported by evidence IDs. Distinguish source quotations from model synthesis: a direct_quote claim cites exactly one evidence item and its text is that item's quote, verbatim; any reworded or combined claim is model_synthesis. Gaps concern the reviewed evidence, not all literature; absent reporting is not proof of absence. Every scientific claim must reference evidence and paper IDs. Do not turn qualitative support categories into calibrated probabilities.
