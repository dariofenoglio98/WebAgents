# Evidence Synthesis

Group papers by meaningful methodological relationships and report differences supported by evidence IDs. Distinguish source quotations from model synthesis: a direct_quote claim cites exactly one evidence item and its text is that item's quote, verbatim; any reworded or combined claim is model_synthesis. Gaps concern the reviewed evidence, not all literature; absent reporting is not proof of absence. Every scientific claim must reference evidence and paper IDs, copied character for character from the supplied evidence items. When the supplied evidence supports no claim about the question, return no themes and no claims rather than writing unsupported ones. Do not turn qualitative support categories into calibrated probabilities.
