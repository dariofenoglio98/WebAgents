WebAgents for macOS

This installs two optional parts of the WebAgents Agent Browser. The Chrome extension works without them.

  • Native helper: lets the extension unlock your keys and records from your login keychain, and report Ollama's state. It does three things only and never receives a provider key, a record or a package.
  • Ollama for local models (you can untick it on the next screen): uses Ollama if it is already installed; otherwise installs it with Homebrew, or downloads the pinned release and checks its SHA-256 and its signature. Then it allows the extension to call Ollama, now and at every login.

Everything goes into /Library/Application Support/WebAgents, Chrome's native-messaging folder and one LaunchAgent. To remove it: sudo "/Library/Application Support/WebAgents/uninstall.sh" (Ollama and your models stay).
